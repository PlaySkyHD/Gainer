The iOS app ships an extract of the Bundeslebensmittelschlüssel (BLS) 4.0 in
`apps/mobile/assets/food/bls_4_0.json`: Max Rubner-Institut (2025):
Bundeslebensmittelschlüssel (BLS), Version 4.0,
https://doi.org/10.25826/Data20251217-134202-0, licensed under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The extract contains code,
name and energy, protein, carbohydrate, fat, fiber and salt per 100 g, rounded
to two decimals; it is generated by `apps/mobile/tool/extract_bls.py`. The app
names the source on every result and on its „Lizenzen“ page.

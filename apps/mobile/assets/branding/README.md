# Gainer app icon artwork

`gainer-app-icon.png` is the approved, opaque square master artwork used for
the iOS AppIcon asset catalog. It was generated on 2026-10-14 with the built-in
image-generation tool for the Gainer app. The provided 1254×1254 RGB PNG is
preserved here; catalog PNGs are deterministic Lanczos downscales of this
master, converted to RGB PNG without transparency. No repainting or other
creative edits were applied.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: logo-design. Create one production iOS app icon for Gainer, a personal strength training and nutrition tracking app. Square 1024x1024 artwork filling the entire canvas edge to edge with opaque deep midnight navy background. One bold centered distinctive geometric capital G monogram in luminous fresh mint green, its short horizontal crossbar subtly shaped like a compact dumbbell, integrated into the letter not an additional object. Strong clean silhouette, generous 18 percent breathing room, instantly readable at 40 pixels. Restrained premium Apple-platform visual craft, smooth precise edges with very subtle satin depth and gentle tonal background gradient. No other letters, no app name, no small details, no border, no inset icon tile, no rounded canvas corners, no phone or store mockup, no watermark, no transparency. Export full square icon artwork, not presentation.
